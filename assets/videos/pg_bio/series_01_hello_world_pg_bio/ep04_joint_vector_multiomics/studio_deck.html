Ep 4: The Multiomics Holy Grail — Vectors & Live UniProt

⚙️ Systems Engineering Lens: 1280-D halfvec HNSW cosine distance (<=>) narrows 2.34M proteins down to 15 candidates in 0.4 ms, short-circuiting into O(N) two-row Smith-Waterman DP and enriching winners live via bio_fetch_uniprot().

1. In-Database SQL Pipeline

bio_demo=# WITH candidates AS (
    SELECT uniprot_id, sequence, (embedding <=> :bait_vec) AS cos_dist
    FROM dark_proteome ORDER BY embedding <=> :bait_vec LIMIT 10
)
SELECT c.uniprot_id, u.organism, round(c.cos_dist::numeric, 4) AS dist,
       smith_waterman_score(c.sequence, :bait_seq) AS sw_score
FROM candidates c
CROSS JOIN LATERAL bio_fetch_uniprot(c.uniprot_id) u;
